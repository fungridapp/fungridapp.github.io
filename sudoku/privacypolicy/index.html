Who we are

Sudoku Mini is made by fungrid, an independent app developer. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean fungrid, and "the app" means Sudoku Mini for Android. We are the data controller for the limited processing described here.

Questions about privacy: fungridapp@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or device identifiers, and we do not build a profile of you. We receive no reports about how you play.

Two things sit outside that, and both are handled by Google rather than by us:

  • Ads, described below.
  • Google Play may give us anonymous, aggregated statistics about installs, uninstalls, ratings and crashes for the app as a whole, if your Android settings allow it. These are counts and technical reports, not information about you, and we cannot identify anyone from them.

Ads

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the menu and the board, a full-screen ad after a lost game, and optional rewarded video ads you choose to watch for an extra hint or a second chance. Ads are never shown over a finished puzzle's celebration or during play.

To serve those ads, Google may process data such as your advertising ID, IP address (which gives approximate, city-level location), device model and operating system version, the app you are using, and your interactions with the ad. Google acts as an independent controller for this. What Google does with it is set out in Google's Privacy Policy and in how Google uses data from sites and apps that use its services.

Your consent

Where the law requires it — in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland — the app shows Google's consent form the first time you open it, and asks whether ads may be personalised. Your answer is remembered, and you can change it at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices inside the app. If you decline, ads still appear, but they are not personalised.

Opting out on your device

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can delete or reset your advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation for every app.
  • Google account holders: My Ad Center.

Sharing a board with a friend

When you share a board, the app creates a link such as https://fungridapp.github.io/sudoku/?b=…. The part after ?b= is the puzzle itself — its size, difficulty, starting clues and, if you have solved it, your time — packed into a short code.

  • The link holds no personal information: no name, no account, nothing identifying you.
  • Nothing is uploaded when you share. Your phone builds the link, and the messaging app you pick sends it, under that app's own privacy policy.
  • Whoever opens the link gets the same board. They cannot see anything else about you or your games.

This website

These pages are static and hosted on GitHub Pages. There are no cookies, no analytics and no trackers.

  • GitHub serves the pages and, like any web host, may log technical details such as your IP address and browser for security and abuse prevention. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • The pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers and Google receives your IP address in doing so.
  • A shared board's code is read by your own browser to draw the board. It is never sent anywhere.

Children

Sudoku Mini is a puzzle game suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Because the app shows ads, a parent or guardian should decide whether it is appropriate for their child. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, write to us and we will help; in practice the app holds nothing that identifies anyone.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data. Clear individual boards or all history on the Stats page. Uninstalling the app removes everything it has stored, permanently.
  • Change your ad choices at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Play without ads loading by switching off your connection; the game works offline.
  • Access, correction, deletion, portability and objection. Under laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA/CPRA you have rights over personal data held about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to hand over or delete. For data processed by Google for ads, use the Google links above or contact Google directly.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information, and never have.

Data is kept only on your device, for as long as you keep the app installed. We run no servers and hold no backups of your data.
